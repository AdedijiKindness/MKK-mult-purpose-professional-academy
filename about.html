About

Knowledge. Skills. Wealth.

MKK Multipurpose Professional Academy (MPA) is a government-registered and professionally accredited institution commited to delivering quality education, practical skills, and professional training in Natural Medicine, Integrative Healthcare, Building Technology, Engineering, and Property Development. Our mission is to equip individuals with the knowledge, skills, and professional competence needed for employment, entrepreneurship, and lifelong career success.

At MKK Multipurpose Professional Academy, we believe education is a pathway to transformation. By combining academic excellence with practical training, we empower our students to become competent professionals, successful entrepreneurs, and responsible leaders who contribute positively to society.

Our Faculty

At MKK Multipurpose Professional Academy (MPA), our faculty comprises experienced professionals, industry experts, and qualified educators dedicated to delivering quality education, practical training, and professional mentorship. Their expertise and commitment ensure that every student receives the knowledge, skills, and guidance needed to excel in their chosen profession.

Natural Medicine & Herbal Science

Learn the principles of natural healing, medicinal plants, herbal preparation, disease prevention, and holistic healthcare.

Fertility Science & Natural Therapy

Study natural approaches to male and female fertility, reproductive health, pregnancy support, and family wellness.

Integrative Medicine

Combine conventional healthcare knowledge with evidence-informed natural therapies to promote holistic patient care.

Healthcare Assistant & Natural Therapy

Develop practical skills in patient care, first aid, health support services, and complementary natural therapeutic practices.

Mental Health, Addiction & Natural Therapy

Study mental health conditions, addiction prevention and recovery, counselling techniques, psychosocial support, and natural therapeutic approaches that promote emotional and psychological well-being.

Caregiving & Natural Therapy

Learn professional caregiving for children, adults, the elderly, and individuals with special needs using holistic care approaches.

Marriage & Family Therapy

Acquire skills in relationship counselling, conflict resolution, family development, parenting support, and emotional wellness.

Civil & Building Construction

Study building construction methods, civil engineering principles, site supervision, construction materials, and project execution.

Architectural & Structural Design

Learn architectural planning, building design, structural analysis, drafting, and construction documentation.

Land & Engineering Survey

Gain practical knowledge in land measurement, mapping, engineering surveys, GIS, GPS technology, and boundary determination.

Property & Project Management

Learn property administration, real estate management, project planning, budgeting, contract administration, and leadership.

Land & Property Dispute Resolution & General Mediation

Develop expertise in land laws, property dispute management, negotiation, general mediation, documentation, and peaceful conflict resolution.
