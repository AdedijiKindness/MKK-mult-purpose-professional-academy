Join NGHPA

Who We Are

Health. Education.
Prosperity.

NGHPA is a registered professional body for certified practitioners and students in natural and integrative healthcare. We promote health education, professional growth, wellness, and prosperity.

Members enjoy CPD training, seminars, workshops, and conferences, along with networking, certification, and induction. We also provide service support, educational and production financial aid, counseling, and the freedom to practice without censure or criticism.

Eligibility

Who Can Join the Association?

Membership is open to a wide range of practitioners, students, and qualification holders in the healthcare space.

01

Certified Natural Medicine Practitioners and Orthodox (Conventional) Healthcare Practitioners who integrate, or wish to integrate, Natural Medicine into their practice.

02

Orthodox Healthcare Practitioners Studying Natural Healthcare-Related Courses.

03

Students or Prospective Students of Natural Medicine or Orthodox Healthcare-Related Courses Who Wish to Integrate Natural Medicine into Their Practice.

04

Holders of Academic or Vocational Qualifications in Natural Medicine or Orthodox Healthcare-Related Professions Who Wish to Integrate Natural Medicine into Their Practice.

05

Practitioners without formal qualifications who are willing to enroll in the Association's training institution for certification — for example, local chemist operators, traditional herbal medicine practitioners, and auxiliary nurses.
